Postponements & Rescheduling

If the Event is postponed, rescheduled, or extended due to venue availability, permitting, weather conditions, inspections, or safety requirements, all tickets will remain valid for the new date(s). Postponed or rescheduled events are not eligible for refunds under any circumstances.

Guest Availability

The Royal Duck USA is not responsible for a ticket holder’s inability to attend the Event. Personal circumstances, including but not limited to travel conflicts, scheduling changes, illness, weather conditions, or other obligations, do not qualify for refunds.

Weather & Safety

For safety reasons, attractions may be delayed, temporarily closed, or closed early without prior notice. In the event of a full Event cancellation (not postponement), ticket holders will receive a credit valid for any future The Royal Duck USA event within twelve (12) months. Credits are non-refundable and have no cash value.

Ticket Transfers

All tickets are fully transferable to another individual at no additional cost. The original purchaser remains responsible for ensuring the recipient complies with all Event terms and conditions.

Chargeback & Payment Disputes

By completing this purchase, I agree not to initiate a chargeback or payment dispute except in cases of unauthorized or fraudulent use of my payment method. Initiating a chargeback for a valid, delivered ticket, or for a postponed (not cancelled) Event, may be considered misuse of the chargeback process. The Royal Duck USA reserves the right to void tickets, revoke any issued credits, and deny current or future access to events in such cases. The company may also provide this agreement and proof of ticket delivery to the payment processor to dispute any chargeback claims.
